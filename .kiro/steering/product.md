# Product — heyismail.com

**heyismail** is the personal website and portfolio of Ismail, a freelance developer who builds custom membership platforms for LinkedIn creators, coaches, and educators.

## Core Value Proposition
Custom-branded community and course platforms delivered in 7 days, with full source code and infrastructure ownership transferred to the client on day 7. Starts at $5,000. No recurring platform fees, no vendor lock-in.

## Target Audience
LinkedIn creators with 50K+ followers who are currently using generic tools (Kajabi, Circle, Notion, Gumroad) and want a platform that matches their brand quality.

## Site Purpose
- Marketing and lead generation for Ismail's development service
- Showcase of completed builds (case studies)
- Booking calls via Cal.com

## Key Pages
- `/` — Homepage with hero, problem/solution, process overview, builds preview, FAQ
- `/builds` — Full case studies for each completed platform
- `/builds/[slug]` — Individual detailed case study
- `/process` — Detailed 7-day delivery process
- `/about` — Ismail's story and principles
- `/contact` — Contact form and booking link

## Brand
- Primary color: `#14A714` (green)
- Dark background: `#06382C` (forest green)
- Light background: `#FBFFFC` (near-white)
- Dark text: `#0F0F0F`
- Font classes: `font-display` (Inter, headings) and `font-body` (Inter, body text)
